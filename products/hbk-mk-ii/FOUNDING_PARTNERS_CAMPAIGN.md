# VVU HBK Founding Partners Campaign Framework

## Core Campaign Narrative

> *"We are not requesting unrestricted funding. We are inviting your organization to sponsor one operational resource that enables the HBK Applied Research Programme to continue building South African technology for water infrastructure."*

### The One-Sentence Ask

> *"Will you become one of the first 100 organizations helping establish South Africa's HBK Applied Research Programme?"*

---

## Campaign Psychology

| Old Approach | New Approach |
|--------------|--------------|
| "Will you sponsor us?" | "Will you become one of the first 100 organizations helping establish South Africa's HBK Applied Research Programme?" |
| Asking for help | Inviting participation |
| One large sponsor needed | Many small contributions build momentum |
| Passive request | Active campaign |
| Uncertainty about what's needed | Clear menu of opportunities |

## Sponsorship Catalogue

### Operations Package — R15,000–30,000
- 2 Desks (University, office provider)
- 2 Chairs (University, office provider)
- 1 Lockable cabinet (University, office provider)
- **Impact:** Enables dedicated research workspace

### Engineering Package — R30,000–90,000
- 1–3 Laptops (Dell, Lenovo, HP)
- 1–3 External monitors (Hardware manufacturer, retail)
- 1–2 UPS units (Makro, Builders)
- 1–3 Keyboard & mouse (Hardware manufacturer, retail)
- **Impact:** Powers engineering development and algorithm validation

### Connectivity Package — R5,000–15,000/year
- 1 Router (MTN, Vodacom)
- 1–2 SIM cards (Telecom provider)
- 200+ GB Monthly data (Telecom provider)
- **Impact:** Enables field data transmission and cloud connectivity

### Field Operations Package — R10,000–25,000
- 3–5 Reflective jackets (PPE supplier)
- 3–5 Safety boots (PPE supplier)
- 3–5 Hard hats (PPE supplier)
- 2–3 Equipment bags (PPE supplier)
- **Impact:** Enables safe field deployment

### Workshop Package — R5,000–15,000/workshop
- Up to 500 pages printing (Local print shop)
- Meeting room as needed (University)
- Coffee/refreshments for 20 (Local café)
- Lunch for 20 (Catering company)
- **Impact:** Enables research workshops and stakeholder meetings

### Branding Package — R8,000–25,000
- 5–10 Branded shirts (Textile company)
- 3–5 Jackets (Textile company)
- 5–10 Caps (Textile company)
- 5–10 Name badges (Signage company)
- 1 Vehicle branding (optional) (Signage company)
- **Impact:** Professional presence and programme identity

## Impact Language Framework

| Need Statement | Impact Statement |
|----------------|------------------|
| "We require office space." | "A contribution of temporary workspace will directly accelerate engineering development, field validation, and student collaboration during the foundational phase of the HBK Applied Research Programme." |
| "We need laptops." | "Sponsoring a workstation enables our engineering team to develop and validate the HBK Mk-II platform, advancing South Africa's hydraulic intelligence capabilities." |
| "We require mobile data." | "A data contribution allows our field teams to transmit critical acoustic and pressure evidence in real-time, accelerating validation of Bayesian leak detection algorithms." |
| "We need branded shirts." | "Supporting field uniforms establishes a professional research presence during municipal site visits, building trust with partners and communities." |
| "We require workshop catering." | "Sponsoring workshop refreshments enables productive collaboration among researchers, municipalities, and industry partners working on water infrastructure solutions." |

## Programme Timeline

### Phase 1: Campaign Launch (Month 1–2)
- Launch Founding 100 campaign
- Approach Tier 1–3 partners
- Secure first commitments
- Establish visible progress

### Phase 2: Momentum Building (Month 3–6)
- Expand to Tier 4–8 partners
- Continue securing commitments
- Begin field operations
- Track and report progress

### Phase 3: Programme Delivery (Month 7–18)
- Deploy HBK Mk-II platforms
- Collect field data
- Publish research findings
- Engage partners in programme activities
