Ubuntu Score

Your reputation,
earned on-chain.

The Ubuntu Score measures your reliability as a pool member — contribution consistency, governance participation, and network velocity. Higher scores unlock better pools and lower platform fees.

On-time contributions amplify your score. Late payments reduce it. Consistent velocity qualifies you for Ubuntu Prime tier.
