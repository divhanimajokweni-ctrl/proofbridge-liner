SEARM1 Enterprise

v2.0 · Infrastructure Intelligence

Overview

SEARM1 is an evidence‑validation and hydraulic observability platform that transforms sparse operational data into confidence‑ranked, spatially verified events. It helps infrastructure operators identify anomalies earlier, reduce investigation cost, and create an auditable chain of evidence.

Core thesis: Water is the first application — not the whole company. The same architecture extends to industrial cooling, data centres, and energy infrastructure.

VVU‑IVE Engine

Validation · Verification · Unification · Independence · Value · Evidence

The VVU‑IVE core combines sparse sensor telemetry, spatial network models, and evidence‑independence scoring to produce a single Evidence Independence Score (EIS) — a 0–1 measure of event confidence.

Evidence Independence Specification

  • Primary evidence – direct sensor readings (flow, pressure, acoustic)
  • Correlated evidence – cross‑sensor consistency checks
  • Independent evidence – spatial / temporal pattern validation
  • Audit trail – SHA‑256 integrity hash for every event
