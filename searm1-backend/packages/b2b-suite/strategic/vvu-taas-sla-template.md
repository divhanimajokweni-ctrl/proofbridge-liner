**TERMINAL-AS-A-SERVICE (TaaS) SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT TEMPLATE**

### 1. CONTRACTUAL BOUNDARIES & REVENUE SPLIT

Under the TaaS leasing structure, hardware ownership is never transferred to the Operator. The financial ledger is structured as a non-dilutive operational expense (OpEx) model governed by a strict 60/30/10 subscription-based revenue split:

| Allocation | Percentage | Operational Scope & Recipient |
|------------|------------|-------------------------------|
| Primary Subscription Base | 60.0% | Allocated to the Provider (Vaguely Vanity LLC) to cover continuous edge-compute licensing and centralised data processing. |
| Sinking Fund & Spares | 30.0% | Retained in a secure hardware preservation fund to cover active terminal replacements and structural spool refurbishments. |
| Field Operations Reserve | 10.0% | Dedicated to local, on-site physical maintenance, including local engineering inspections and coupon extraction. |

### 2. HARDWARE AND TELEMETRY PERFORMANCE TARGETS

The Provider guarantees a highly continuous operational envelope for the edge hardware, backed by the robust thermal and power engineering of the HBK Mk-II assembly:

| Metric | Target | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **System Uptime** | ≥ 99.5% | The edge-compute terminal (powered by the AMD Kria K26 SoM) shall maintain an operational duty cycle of ≥ 99.5% per billing cycle, excluding pre-scheduled maintenance windows. |
| **Precision Alarm Metric** | FPR ≤ 5.0% | The Hydro-Bayesian Kernel (HBK) must maintain a False Positive Rate of ≤ 5.0% by utilizing its joint Poisson-Gaussian mixture noise models to filter out heavy industrial and mining plant vibration. |
| **Measurement Sensitivity** | SNR ≥ 45 dB | The TI PCM1864-Q1 ADC sensor array must operate with an acoustic Signal-to-Noise Ratio of ≥ 45 dB, preserved via strict star-ground electrical isolating paths. |
| **Autonomous Thermal Watchdog** | ≤ 65°C | The system's passive phase-change cooling must maintain the edge SoC below the 65°C watchdog limit. If temperatures exceed 75°C, the system will execute wake-on-acoustic interrupt routing to protect battery chemistry. |

### 3. PHYSICAL INTEGRITY DEPLOYMENT (PDU TRACKING)

To bypass administrative and theoretical validation delays, all material corrosion allowances must be physically audited in real-time. The Operator agrees to the continuous inline deployment of the Passive Dummy Unit (PDU) spool fitted with the PDU-CC-IND-01 PEEK Coupon Clamp Bracket:

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Coupon Extraction Frequency** | Extraction and gravimetric mass logging of the specimen coupons (316L, 304, Galvanized Steel, and HDPE) must be executed at strict 90-calendar-day intervals (±3 days) in accordance with the 4-step SOP. |
| **Analysis Standards** | Mass-loss logging must be performed on laboratory scales with ≥ 0.1 mg precision. Annualized corrosion rates must be calculated using the ASTM G31 formula and logged in the parent database. |
| **Warning Thresholds** | If an annualized corrosion rate exceeds 0.10 mm/yr, or a localized pitting depth exceeds 0.50 mm, the system will automatically raise an integrity flag in the digital twin's predictive model. |

### 4. SECURITY, DATA SOVEREIGNTY, AND AUDIT TRAILS

To protect proprietary trade secrets and system integrity, data exchange is subject to strict software-governance policies:

- **Data Ownership**: Raw physical sensor signals and localized state estimation data streams remain the exclusive intellectual property of the Provider. The Operator receives a non-transferable, read-only license to view processed diagnostic dashboards.
- **Cryptographic Provenance**: Every verified state claim and leak localization report generated by the edge node is cryptographically signed with an SHA-256 hash in Write-Once-Read-Many (WORM) storage, ensuring absolute auditability.

### 5. SERVICE CREDIT AND LIABILITY CAP

In the event of an unscheduled compute outage that drops system uptime below the 99.5% threshold, the Operator shall be credited a pro-rated percentage of the monthly subscription fee. Under no circumstances shall the Provider's total liability exceed 100% of the active monthly subscription fee for the affected node. The Provider is not liable for structural pipeline damage occurring outside the active, verified telemetry zones.
