### 4. PHYSICAL-TO-DIGITAL VALIDATION PROTOCOLS (PDU)

To preserve our strict Zero Fabrication Policy for active electronics while establishing physical, empirical data in high-acid or corrosive industrial fluid lines, we are initiating the Passive Dummy Unit (PDU) deployment program unilaterally. We do not require municipal access permits or academic research board approvals to deploy test skids inside private industrial pipelines.

Our newly designed **PDU-CC-IND-01 Coupon Clamp Bracket**, CNC-machined from virgin, chemically inert Polyether ether ketone (PEEK), will be placed inside a bypass spool directly on-site at private customer loops. This non-electrical device holds dissimilar metal coupons (316L, 304, and Galvanized Steel) in precise relative coordinate slots, insulated completely by the PEEK walls to eliminate galvanic interference.

By measuring the physical mass-loss and pitting depths of these coupons at 30, 60, and 90-day intervals, our engineers can calculate localized chemical and corrosive rates (SANS 1200 / ISO 9223). This data is ingested as primary evidence into the EIS v1.0 scoring engine. The physical material wear is translated into digital, auditable evidence proving the decay of the client's asset, establishing our digital twin's predictive credibility.
