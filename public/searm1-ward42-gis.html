SEARM1 Ward 42 Scene — Simulation

This build is an ArcGIS Scene Viewer-inspired presentation layer, not an embedded ArcGIS Online scene.

  • Terrain is procedurally generated between 52 m and 232 m.
  • Roads, parcels, buildings, nodes and pipes are synthetic scene objects.
  • Water-network geometry is positioned below the terrain to create a true subsurface view.
  • Code City towers remain linked to repository-style folder/file metadata.
  • Leak mode is a deterministic visual simulation on PIP3.
  • Real Esri, OSM, TomTom, NASA/USGS or municipal layers require their actual services/data and licensing; they are not silently substituted here.
