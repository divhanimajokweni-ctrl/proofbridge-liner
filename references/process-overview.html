05

Patterns of the Top 10%

InsightsBest Practices

Pattern 1: Above-the-Fold Clarity

All top-scoring sites communicate their value proposition within the first 3 seconds. The headline + subheadline + CTA triad is complete above the fold, with no scrolling required to understand what they do.

Pattern 2: Social Proof at First Interaction

Top sites place testimonials, client logos, or trust badges within the first viewport. They don't wait for the user to scroll to the bottom.

Pattern 3: Distinctive Visual Identity

Market leaders use a unique color palette and typography system. They don't look like templates — they look branded.

Pattern 4: Clear, Persistent CTA

The primary CTA is always visible (sticky nav or floating button) and uses action-oriented language ("Get my free audit" vs "Submit").
