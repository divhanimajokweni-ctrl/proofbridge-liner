# Ant Stack Onboarding Flow
## For Ubuntu Pools Members

### Step 1: Join a Pool Match

1. Open Ubuntu Pools dashboard
2. Click "Join Match" on active pool
3. Select stake tier (R50, R100, R250, R500)
4. Confirm Stitch payment
5. Wait for match to start

### Step 2: Play the Match

**Colony Players:**
- Reveal tiles to find workers and soldiers
- Vote to seal sectors (protect resources)
- Plant decoys to misdirect anteater

**Anteater Player:**
- Plan strikes based on revealed tiles
- Devour workers and soldiers
- Adapt strategy as colony responds

### Step 3: After the Match

- View match results
- See payouts credited to your pool contribution
- Check updated Trust Factor
- Review match audit log

### Step 4: Keep Playing

- Join more matches to increase Trust Factor
- Higher Trust Factor = better matchmaking
- Compete on leaderboard
- Invite friends to join pool
