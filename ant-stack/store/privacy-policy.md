# Privacy Policy — Ant Stack: Food Chain
## VVU Foundation · Gqeberha, Eastern Cape

### 1. Data We Collect
- **Anonymous Player ID**: UUID generated per session, not linked to personal identity
- **Trust Factor**: Aggregated reputation score based on match history
- **Match History**: Anonymized match results, stakes, and outcomes
- **Telemetry**: Aggregated heatmap data (4x4 sectors), not per-player

### 2. Data We Do NOT Collect
- Real names, email addresses, or phone numbers
- Financial information (all stakes are handled by Ubuntu Pools)
- Location data beyond region (af-south-1)
- Device identifiers or hardware fingerprints

### 3. Data Retention
- Match audit logs: 30 days
- Anonymized match history: Indefinite for leaderboard purposes
- Trust Factor: Persistent per anonymous ID

### 4. Data Minimisation
- All data is pseudonymized by default
- No cross-referencing between matches
- Aggregated telemetry only (4x4 sectors)

### 5. Your Rights (POPIA §18)
- Right to access: Request your match history
- Right to correction: Dispute a match result
- Right to erasure: Delete your anonymized profile
- Right to object: Opt-out of leaderboard
