---

## Hard Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Never mix personal and business funds** | Clean transaction history = bank trust = unsecured credit lines later |
| **IP lives in the company, not with you** | Your company must legally own the software. When you sell equity or move to a trust, the value is in the company's asset column — safe from personal creditors. |
| **Pay yourself a minimal baseline salary** | Only what you need to survive + service personal debt. Everything else stays in the business as retained earnings, taxed at the lower corporate rate. |
