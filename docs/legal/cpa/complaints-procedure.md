# Complaints Procedure

## Scope

All Ubuntu Pools members. Required under Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 before first paying member.

## Complaints Contact

- **Email:** complaints@vvu.co.za
- **Post:** `[TO BE SUPPLIED]`
- **In person:** `[TO BE SUPPLIED]`

## Procedure

### Step 1: Internal Resolution (7 days)

Member submits complaint in writing to complaints@vvu.co.za. VVU acknowledges within 2 business days and resolves within 5 business days.

### Step 2: Escalation (7 days)

If unresolved, complaint reviewed by Compliance Officer. Response within 3 business days.

### Step 3: External (if unresolved)

- Ombudsman / industry tribunal
- National Consumer Commission
- Magistrates Court (small claims or equivalent)

## Complaint Register

Maintained by Compliance Officer. Reviewed quarterly at governance meetings.

## SLA Summary

| Stage | SLA |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | 2 business days |
| Internal resolution | 5 additional business days |
| Escalation response | 3 business days |
| External escalation | As per third party |

## Required Before Launch

Prior to accepting first paying member:
- complaints@vvu.co.za address operational
- Internal dispute resolution person designated
- Governance meeting minutes record approval of this procedure
