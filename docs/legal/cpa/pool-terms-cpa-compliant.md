# CPA-Compliant Pool Terms

## Scope

All Ubuntu Pools membership agreements. These terms must be provided to every member before or at time of contract conclusion.

## CPA Obligations

### Plain Language
All terms must be written in plain, understandable language. Technical blockchain terminology must be explained or accompanied by a glossary.

### Disclosures Required

| Disclosure | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Pool contribution amount | Fixed and known at time of agreement |
| Payout schedule | Defined; not variable without formal process |
| Cooling-off right | Right to cancel within 5 business days |
| Dispute resolution procedure | Internal + external (Consumer Goods and Services Ombud equivalent) |

### Cooling-Off Right

Members may cancel membership without penalty within **5 business days** of contract conclusion, provided no payouts have been triggered.
