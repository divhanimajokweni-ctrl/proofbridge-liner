## Request Procedure

### How to Submit

- **Email:** privacy@vvu.co.za
- **Post:** `[TO BE SUPPLIED]`
- **In person:** `[TO BE SUPPLIED]`

### Required in Request

- Full name and ID number or passport
- Contact details
- Description of record requested
- Form in which access requested

### Response Time

- 30 days standard
- Complex requests may be extended to 60 days with written notice

### Fees

No fee for personal record requests. Reasonable fee may be charged for copies exceeding 100 pages.

## Categories of Records

### Automatically Available
- PAIA manual itself
- Publicly available marketing materials
- Land-page content

### Restricted — Requires Justification
- Membership records
- Contribution/payment records
- Audit logs
- Configuration for platform operations

### Not Disclosable
- Trade secrets
- Commercial information in pre-launch phase
- Personal information of other members

## Grounds for Refusal

- Manifestly frivolous or vexatious
- Request endangers safety or security
- Request would unreasonably affect commercial interests

## Appeals

Appeals to Information Regulator:  
https://www.justice.gov.za/inforeg/

## Manual Publication

This manual must be published on the VV Ubuntu Labs website before commercial operation commences.
