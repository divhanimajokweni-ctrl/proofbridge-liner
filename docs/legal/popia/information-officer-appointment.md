## Required Before Commercial Operation

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), VV Ubuntu Labs LLC must designate an Information Officer before processing personal information at commercial scale.

## Responsibilities

- Ensure compliance with POPIA conditions for lawful processing
- Receive and respond to data subject requests
- Maintain Records of Processing (ROP)
- Conduct or commission Privacy Impact Assessments (PIAs)
- Report personal data breaches to the Information Regulator within 72 hours
