## Rights

### 1. Right of Access
Data subjects may request a copy of all personal data held. Respond within one month (extendable to two months for complex requests).

### 2. Right to Correction
Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data. Update in Supabase database; propagate to recalculation services.

### 3. Right to Erasure
Delete personal data except where retention is legally required (contribution/payment records under FICA/Tax Act).

### 4. Right to Objection
Suspend processing where legal basis is alegitimate interest. Do not apply to processing required by law.

### 5. Right to Portability
Provide machine-readable export of structured personal data upon request.

## Procedure Steps

1. Receive request at privacy@vvu.co.za
2. Log in audit log within 24 hours
3. Verify identity against Supabase Auth
4. Execute in database within SLA
5. Issue written confirmation

## Breach Notification

Personal data breaches must be reported to the Information Regulator within 72 hours of discovery.
