A document describing a token supply model, staking mechanics, and reward rates, when circulated to third parties, may constitute a **public financial product offer** under:

- FAIS Act Section 7
- Draft Crypto-Asset Regulatory Framework (FSCA Consultation Paper CP 112 of 2024)

The current disclaimer in fundraising materials ("under research", "not available for purchase") is not legally sufficient for this purpose.
