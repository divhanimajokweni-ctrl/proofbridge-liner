# FSCA Crypto-Asset Regulatory Framework Analysis

## Applicability to Ubuntu Pools / ProofBridge Liner

### Current Regulatory Position

The FSCA has adopted a "risk-based" approach to crypto-asset service providers (CASPs). Ubuntu Pools, by virtue of operating a pooled contribution mechanism with on-chain receipt anchoring via ProofBridge, likely triggers CASP classification.

### Assessment Summary

| Risk Area | Determination | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Tokenised receipt issuance via ProofBridge | Likely CASP activity | Confirm legal opinion before launch |
| Pooled fund management by Ubuntu Pools | Collective investment scheme risk | Obtain FSP licence or confirm exemption |
| Staking/reward mechanics (if any) | Potential investment product | Exclude from MVP or obtain approval |

### Condition for MVP Launch

- No marketing of PBX token to retail investors until FSP licence issued
- Internal receipts only until regulatory clarity obtained
- All external communications to carry appropriate risk disclaimers reviewed by legal counsel
