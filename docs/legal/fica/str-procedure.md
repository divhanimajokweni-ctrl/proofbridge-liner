# Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR) Procedure

## Reporting Obligation

Under FICA, VV Ubuntu Labs LLC must report suspicious or unusual transactions to the Financial Intelligence Centre without delay.

## What is a Reportable Transaction

A transaction is reportable if it:

- Has no apparent economic or lawful purpose
- May be related to money laundering or terrorist financing
- Involves a member whose stated circumstances are inconsistent with expected behaviour
- Follows a pattern inconsistent with the member's profile

## STR Submission

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Recipient | Financial Intelligence Centre (AUSTRAC) |
| Method | AUSTRAC online portal |
| Timing | Without delay upon becoming aware |
| Who may submit | Compliance Officer only |

## Do Not Tip Off

Never inform the member that a report has been or will be made. Tipping off is a criminal offence under FICA.

## Escalation Path

1. Staff or system identifies suspicious activity
2. Flag in admin panel with `suspicious-activity` tag
3. Compliance Officer reviews within 24 hours
4. Compliance Officer decides to submit or close
5. Internal log updated; report filed if submitted

## Review
