# Customer Due Diligence Procedures

## Applicability

All Ubuntu Pools members and administrators.

## Know Your Member (KYM)

| Verification Step | Method | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Identity document | SA ID / Passport scan | Yes |
| Contact verification | Email + phone OTP | Yes |
| Bank account linkage | Third-party verification | Yes |
| PEP/Sanctions screening | Government/AUSTRAC list | Yes |
| Address confirmation | Utility bill / proof of residence | Above threshold |

## Threshold-Based Enhanced CDD

CDD is enhanced for contributions above R50,000 in a single calendar year.

Enhanced steps:
- Source of funds declaration
- Beneficial ownership confirmation
- Senior management approval

## Ongoing Monitoring

- Annual re-verification of identity documents
- Watchlist rescreening on regulatory list updates
- Contribution pattern anomaly review quarterly

## Records

CDD records retained for 5 years after account closure in accordance with FICA requirements.

## Non-Compliance Handling

Failure to complete CDD results in suspended membership until verification is complete.
